Convert AVIF to JPG in your browser

Choose or drop AVIF files, the output is preset to JPG. Conversion runs on your device; the files are not uploaded. You can convert several files at once and download them one by one or as a zip.

AVIF is decoded by your browser's built-in decoder (supported in current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari versions). JPG has no transparency, so transparent areas become white. Other formats are accepted too; you can change the output format below.

Choose files or drop here

Converted in your browser. Nothing is uploaded. Accepts HEIC, HEIF, WebP, AVIF, PNG, JPEG.

HEIC/HEIF: only the first image in a file is converted. EXIF and orientation are dropped. If your browser cannot open HEIC natively, the decoder loads on first use and may take a few seconds.

Add images to enable Convert. ZIP download is available for 2 or more converted files.

    Limitations and browser support · only the first image in a HEIC/HEIF file is converted; EXIF and orientation are dropped; Safari/iOS not fully tested yet

    Limitations: for HEIC/HEIF files with several images (bursts, live photos, sequences) only the first/primary image is converted. EXIF metadata (camera data, GPS location, date) is not kept, and EXIF orientation is not applied, so some photos may come out rotated.

    Browser testing: tested in Chrome/Chromium, Firefox and a WebKit engine build. Safari on macOS and iOS/iPadOS is not fully tested yet; if something does not work there, please report it on GitHub.
