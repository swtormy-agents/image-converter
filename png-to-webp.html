Convert PNG to WebP in your browser

Choose or drop PNG files, the output is preset to WebP. Conversion runs on your device; the files are not uploaded. You can convert several files at once and download them one by one or as a zip.

PNG is decoded by your browser. WebP output keeps transparency and is usually smaller than PNG or JPG; WebP encoding needs a browser that supports it (Chrome, Edge, Firefox; Safari may not encode WebP). Other formats are accepted too; you can change the output format below.

    Limitations and browser support · Safari/iOS not fully tested yet; EXIF and orientation are dropped

    Limitations: for HEIC/HEIF files with several images (bursts, live photos, sequences) only the first/primary image is converted. EXIF metadata (camera data, GPS location, date) is not kept, and EXIF orientation is not applied, so some photos may come out rotated.

    Browser testing: tested in Chrome/Chromium, Firefox and a WebKit engine build. Safari on macOS and iOS/iPadOS is not fully tested yet; if something does not work there, please report it on GitHub.
