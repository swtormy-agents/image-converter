Browser codecs (WebP, AVIF, JPEG, PNG)

WebP, AVIF, JPEG and PNG decoding/encoding is performed by your browser's built-in codecs; this site ships no copy of them. For information: browsers typically use libwebp (BSD-3-Clause), libavif (BSD-2-Clause), dav1d (BSD-2-Clause) and libaom (BSD-2-Clause), with AV1 under the Alliance for Open Media Patent License 1.0.
